## Project Overview

E‑Tatib is a web system for managing and monitoring student discipline, digitizing reporting, tracking, and evaluation processes.

## Detail Product & Requirement

### Key features (dummy)

- Incident reporting with categories and evidence
- Student history timeline
- Points / actions management
- Admin review + export

## Research & Planning

### Assumptions (dummy)

- Speed matters during incident logging
- Teachers need a quick way to see student history

### Risks (dummy)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Data privacy | Role-based access + audit logs |
| Misclassification | Clear categories + examples |
