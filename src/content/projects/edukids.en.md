## Project Overview
![Cover](/images/edukids/edukids-cover.png "w=360")

EduKids is a mobile learning platform designed for children aged 5–18. In this ecosystem, parents act as decision-makers, while children are the primary users. 

| My Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| UX Researcher & Product Designer | -  Conducted qualitative user research<br> - Synthesized insights into key problem areas<br>- Defined product direction and design principles<br>- Designed user flows and overall experience structure |

## Problem Statement & Goals

### The Problem

1. Parents want their children to learn beyond school, but existing platforms don’t fully support their needs.
2. Parents want their children to learn independently, but they don’t trust digital platforms to be safe, structured, or effective.

Most available solutions:
- Are not well curated  
- Require too much parental involvement  
- Struggle to keep children engaged over time  

### The Goal

Design a learning experience that:

- Enables children to learn independently  
- Gives parents a strong sense of trust and control  

## User Research & Insights

### User Research

I conducted qualitative research to understand how parents approach digital learning for their children.

Key findings:

- Parents want learning that is flexible but still structured  
- Safety is a major concern when using online platforms  
- Most parents don’t have time or knowledge to guide learning directly  
- Clear progress tracking increases trust and commitment

## Requirements

To translate research insights into actionable design decisions, I defined a set of requirements and guiding principles.


### User Requirements

1. Parents need a safe and curated learning environment for their children  
2. Parents need clear visibility into their child’s learning progress  
3. Children need engaging and interactive content to stay motivated  
4. Children need guidance when learning independently  


### System Requirements
 
1. The system should track and visualize learning progress clearly  
2. The system should support gamification to maintain engagement  
3. The system should provide guided assistance (e.g., AI-based support)

### Core Features

![showcase](/images/edukids/edukids-showcase.png)

![2](/images/edukids/edukids1.gif)

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Safe Learning Mode** | Curated and age-appropriate content to ensure children are exposed only to safe and relevant materials |
| **Parent Dashboard** | Provides clear visibility into learning progress, activity, and personalized recommendations |
| **Gamified Learning Path** | Uses points, levels, and rewards to keep children motivated and consistent in learning |
| **AI Mentor Assistant** | Helps guide children through learning without requiring constant parental involvement |
| **Progress Visualization** | Displays learning progress in a simple and easy-to-understand format for parents |
| **Weekly Parent Digest** | Sends automatic summaries so parents stay informed with minimal effort |



### Usability Considerations

Several key decisions shaped the experience:

- Short-form content reduces fatigue and improves engagement  
- Separating parent and child interfaces reduces complexity  
- Clear progress tracking builds trust quickly  
- Gamification supports consistency, not just engagement
